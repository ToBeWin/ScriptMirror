## Description

Speak naturally. Stay focused on the camera.

Teleprompter: ScriptMirror helps you record clear, confident videos without
losing your place. Add a script, set a comfortable pace, and read while you
record with your iPhone or iPad camera.

MAKE EVERY TAKE FEEL NATURAL

• Write a script or import a text file
• Organize, edit, and reorder your scripts
• Adjust text size, spacing, and background for comfortable reading
• Choose timed scrolling or move through your lines manually
• Record with the front or rear camera in up to 1080p when supported
• Save finished videos to Photos
• Choose whether the front-camera preview and saved video use mirror mode

PRIVATE, ON-DEVICE VOICE TRACKING

ScriptMirror includes English and Chinese speech-recognition models. Recognition
runs on your device, and your audio and scripts are not uploaded. The app can
follow your delivery and move through lines as you speak. If voice tracking is
unavailable on a device, timed and manual scrolling remain available.

MADE FOR YOUR NEXT RECORDING

Use ScriptMirror for presentations, lessons, product demos, social videos,
practice, and more. Your scripts, settings, and recordings stay under your
control on your device.

No account required. No subscription required.

Camera and microphone access are used only when you start recording. Finished
videos are saved to your Photos library when requested.

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in is required. On first launch, choose the sample script or
create a script, then tap Start Recording and allow camera and microphone access
to review the capture flow. English and Chinese speech recognition runs on the
device. The app stores scripts and settings locally and saves completed videos
to Photos when requested. If voice recognition is unavailable, timed and manual
scrolling are available.

## App privacy answers to verify in App Store Connect

The app and its included SDKs do not collect or transmit user data. Scripts,
camera video, microphone audio, and recognition results are processed or stored
locally. Confirm these answers against the final uploaded binary and any Apple
