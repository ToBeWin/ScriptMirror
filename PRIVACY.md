# ScriptMirror Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

ScriptMirror is an offline-first teleprompter and selfie video recorder. This
policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

## Summary

ScriptMirror does not collect, sell, or upload personal information. Scripts,
recordings, and offline speech-recognition processing stay on your device.

## Information processed on your device

- **Camera:** Used only after you start a recording, to preview and capture
  video.
- **Microphone:** Used only during recording and on-device speech recognition.
- **Photos / media library:** Used to save completed recordings when you ask
  the app to do so.
- **Scripts and settings:** Stored locally on your device so the app can restore
  your work and preferences.
- **Speech recognition:** English and Chinese recognition runs locally with the
  bundled sherpa-onnx models. Audio is not sent to a server.

ScriptMirror does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account login,
cloud sync, or third-party tracking. The app does not request network access
for its core recording or recognition workflow.

## Sharing and retention

ScriptMirror does not transmit your scripts, audio, video, or settings to us or
to third parties. Files remain on your device until you delete them, remove
the app, or manage them through the system Photos/files tools.

## Children

ScriptMirror is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change
takes effect.
